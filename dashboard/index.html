Statistical baseline

How the experiment works

An intentionally small exploratory study, separate from the frozen EXP-001 foundation-model protocol.

01 · Generate

24 reproducible 60-day return series. Half have a sustained volatility increase after day 30; the others have unchanged volatility with an isolated outlier.

02 · Evaluate

Muse receives only the return observations and returns a change probability, a binary decision and a short reason. A fixed robust statistical rule serves as the baseline.

03 · Interpret

Compare accuracy and Brier score; inspect unsupported numerical claims and probability/decision disagreements. No inference about causal market mechanisms is warranted.
